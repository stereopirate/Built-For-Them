Meet the Spin Board.

A DIY spinning board for sensory play.

Prototype in progressSensory & vestibular playFloor-level

The Spin Board is a sturdy wooden board on a low, rotating base. Your child sits or lies on top while a grown-up slowly turns it.

Spinning boards are a common tool in occupational therapy sensory programs. Slow, controlled spinning gives vestibular input - the movement and balance information the inner ear sends to the brain - which therapists use to support balance, body awareness, and regulation. This version is built from solid pine with rounded edges and built-in hand-hold cutouts, and it sits low to the floor.

Use it with your therapist's guidance

Spinning is strong sensory input. Always have an adult turning the board, and ask your child's therapist how fast, how long, and in which positions spinning is right for your child.
