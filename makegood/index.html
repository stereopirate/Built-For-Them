Who they are

About MakeGood.

MakeGood is a New Orleans 501(c)(3) nonprofit that uses 3D printing to design and make free, custom assistive devices for people with disabilities. Since 2021 they've delivered more than 1,600 individualized devices to families at no cost - with the person who has the need leading the design.

The organization

MakeGood

Designs open-source assistive devices and shares the files, instructions, and education so makers anywhere can produce them.

The build

What is the Toddler Mobility Trainer?

The TMT is a modular, fully 3D-printed wheelchair for children roughly 2 to 8 years old with limited mobility. It's designed to build independence, coordination, and social connection - letting kids explore and keep up with the world around them.

Modular

Snaps together

Assembles without tools or glue. Parts are designed for standard desktop 3D printers.

Repairable

Reprint, don't replace

If a part breaks or wears out, just that piece gets reprinted - not the whole chair.

Open source

Shared freely

MakeGood publishes the full model files and assembly instructions so makers everywhere can help.
